Privacy Policy

App: Pootu Study Companion
Last updated: 28 September 2026

Pootu Study Companion (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps you track study subjects and topics, schedule revisions, manage routines, and optionally use a voice study assistant. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — email address and password when you create an account or sign in.
  • Study content — subjects, topics, revision schedules and progress, class routines, and daily routines that you create in the app.
  • Voice assistant (optional) — if you use the microphone or voice features, audio and related speech text are processed to understand your request and respond. You can skip these features and use the app without them.
  • Notifications & reminders — if you allow notifications, we schedule reminders for revisions, classes, and routine blocks. If you allow push notifications on the web app, we store the information needed to deliver those notifications.
  • Device & local data — settings such as language and theme, and widget display data kept on your device.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, or device calendar. We do not collect precise location.

2. How we use information

  • Create your account and sign you in
  • Store and sync your study data
  • Calculate revision schedules, progress, and streaks
  • Show widgets and send reminders you asked for
  • Provide voice assistance when you choose to use it
  • Keep the service secure and working properly
  • Respond to support and privacy requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your study content for advertising.

3. Sharing of information

We share information only as needed to run the app:

  • Service providers that help us operate the app — for example secure account login, cloud storage and sync, voice processing when you use voice features, and hosting. They may process information only to provide those services.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, legal process, or to protect users and the service.
  • Business transfer — if the app is ever transferred to another operator, information may transfer with it under the same or equivalent protections.

Voice features use third-party AI technology to process speech and generate replies when you use them.

4. Data retention

We keep account and study information while your account is active so the app can work across devices. You can sign out in Settings. To delete your account and associated data, contact us (Section 8). Uninstalling the app removes local data on that device. We may keep limited records longer where required by law, for security, or to prevent abuse.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children

The app is for students and learners and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Skip voice features if you do not want speech processed
  • Turn off notifications in device or browser settings
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information
  • Request a copy of your data where the law requires it

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA/CPRA, including the right to object to or restrict certain processing and to complain to a data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us using Section 8.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests (including account deletion), use the support / developer email published on this app’s Google Play Store listing, or the contact shown in the app’s Settings / About section.

Website: https://pootustudycompanion.xyz

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.
