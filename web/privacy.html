Privacy Policy

App: AI Powered Study Companion (Smart Revision Tracker)
Last updated: 17 September 2026

This app helps you track study subjects, topics, spaced revisions, class and daily routines, optional voice assistance, and reminders. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the AI Powered Study Companion mobile and web app. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email listed in the app’s Play Store listing or About screen.

2. Data we collect

  • Account & sign-in — email address and Google account identifier when you sign in (Google Sign-In or email). Used only to authenticate you and sync your data.
  • Study content — subjects, topics, notes, first-read status, revision schedules and completion, class routines, and daily routines you create. Stored so the app can show progress and reminders.
  • Voice & audio (optional) — if you use the voice assistant, microphone audio and transcribed text may be processed to answer study questions and create/update your study data. You can avoid this by not using the mic features.
  • Device & app data — local preferences (language, settings, widget data) on your device. Home-screen widgets store a copy of routine/revision summaries in app-local storage.
  • Notifications — we schedule local reminders for revisions, classes, and routine blocks. Notification content stays on your device.

3. How we use data

  • Provide account login and cloud sync of your study data
  • Compute revision schedules, streaks, and progress stats
  • Show home-screen widgets and send reminder notifications
  • Run the optional AI voice assistant (if you enable it)
  • Keep the service secure and prevent abuse

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting for your account and study data. See Supabase’s privacy documentation for their processing terms.
  • Google Sign-In — optional login. Google processes your identity under Google’s policies.
  • Google Gemini / generative AI — optional voice assistant features may send your prompts (and related context you choose to share) to Google’s Gemini API to generate replies and tool actions.
  • Image search (optional) — when auto-attaching subject cover images, a search service may be queried with the subject name.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your study content for advertising.

5. Storage & retention

Data is kept while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account or study content where the app supports it, we remove or anonymize that data from our systems, subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Security

Data in transit uses HTTPS. Access to production systems is limited to the developer/operator. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we work to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The app is intended for students and lifelong learners. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices & rights

  • Use email vs Google sign-in as offered in the app
  • Skip voice features if you do not want audio processed
  • Turn off notifications in system settings
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your account data via the contact email

Depending on your location (e.g. EEA, UK, California), you may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.

10. Contact

Privacy requests: use the support email published on this app’s Google Play listing, or the contact shown in the app’s Settings / About section.
