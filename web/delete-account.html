How to request deletion

  1. Enter the email address used for your account in the form above.
  2. Select Delete my account.
  3. We receive your request and begin verification.
Deletion requests are only processed for the account owner. If we cannot verify the request, we may ask you to confirm from the registered email address before deleting anything.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Profile information (email, display name)
  • Study content you created — subjects, topics, revision schedules and progress, class routines, daily routines
  • Notification and push-notification records linked to your account
  • Other personal data tied to your account

What may be kept

  • Limited records required by law, or for security, fraud, and abuse prevention
  • Aggregated or de-identified data that no longer identifies you
  • Encrypted backups for a short period until overwritten in the normal course (typically up to 90 days)

Retention period

After we verify your request, deletion is completed within 30 days (often sooner). Backup copies are purged on the normal backup rotation, typically within 90 days.

Delete data without deleting your account

You can also request deletion or correction of specific data (for example one subject or routine) without closing your account. Use the same form and mention what you want removed in a follow-up email to the contact below. Those requests are handled within 30 days.

Before you continue

  • Account deletion is permanent. Your study history cannot be restored.
  • If you only want to stop using the app, sign out in Settings instead.
  • If you only want to stop reminders, turn off notifications in device or browser settings.
